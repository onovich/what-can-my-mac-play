# Homepage CrossOver picks

Reviewed: 2026-09-05. The homepage helps players discover Steam games without an official Mac edition that can be played through a supported compatibility route. Official Mac editions remain searchable fallback information. Add the two selections below alongside Noita.

## Selection rule

Both candidates have a game-specific CodeWeavers Mac rating of **Runs Great**, a named recent tested CrossOver version and a complete single-player activity. This supports an editorial recommendation to use CrossOver. It does not establish a Mac minimum specification, universal compatibility, a frame-rate target, or a guarantee for every game mode. No extra game-specific blocker appears in the reviewed overview records. That observation does not establish that no blocker exists.

Steam support is independent: Windows-only Steam requirements establish that no official Steam Mac edition is listed under the product rule. Keep the Windows game route supported through CrossOver while marking the official Mac route unsupported. Do not turn the Windows hardware requirements into Mac requirements.

The CodeWeavers overview pages expose both install-button and manual-install text in their static representation. This research therefore does **not** claim a verified one-click installer or invent bottle/backend settings. The tool is paid with a trial; game ownership remains separate. A plain route action is sufficient until an actual installation walkthrough is verified.

## DARK SOULS: REMASTERED — Steam appId 570940

- Store: [Steam listing](https://store.steampowered.com/app/570940/DARK_SOULS_REMASTERED/). Its age gate prevented direct content retrieval; Valve's [technical support page](https://help.steampowered.com/en/wizard/HelpWithGameTechnicalIssue?appid=570940) supplies the store metadata and requirements.

- Compatibility route: [CodeWeavers game record](https://www.codeweavers.com/compatibility/crossover/dark-souls-remastered). Mac rating **Runs Great**, last tested **26.1.0 (2)**. CodeWeavers app identifier is **16729**, not the Steam appId. The Linux rating is separately flagged old and is not used.

- Editorial scope: recommend the Remastered game's single-player exploration/combat through CrossOver. Do not confuse it with Prepare to Die Edition. The reviewed record does not justify declaring multiplayer broken or recommending against the whole purchase merely because multiplayer was not independently inspected.

## Rendering guidance

Show the game, gameplay reason and CrossOver route concisely. Keep exact rated versions, source URLs and audit caveats in internal evidence. Display separate tool cost in details. Do not add “unverified multiplayer” warnings to every single-player recommendation, and do not write “works perfectly”, “all Macs”, “one click”, or a frame rate unsupported by actual measurements.
